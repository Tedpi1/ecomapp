# Let's Shop

**Let's Shop** is a web-based product distribution platform designed to make shopping easier and more convenient for customers.

Instead of requiring customers to physically visit shops to purchase products, Let's Shop enables a company to **bring products directly to customers**. Customers can browse available products, place orders, and have their shopping delivered to them.

## Project Objective

The main objective of Let's Shop is to simplify the traditional shopping and product distribution process by connecting a company directly with its customers through an online platform.

The platform aims to:

- Allow customers to browse products online.
- Allow customers to place orders without visiting a physical shop.
- Enable the company to receive and manage customer orders.
- Make product distribution and delivery more organized.
- Reduce the need for customers to physically travel to shops.
- Provide customers with a convenient and accessible shopping experience.
- Help the company efficiently manage products and customer purchases.

## How It Works

The platform follows a simple process:

1. **Customer visits Let's Shop**
2. **Customer browses available products**
3. **Customer selects the products they want**
4. **Customer places an order**
5. **The company receives the order**
6. **The company prepares the products**
7. **The products are delivered to the customer**

This creates a direct connection between the company and its customers.
